Men’s Shorts Trends You Need to Know in 2025

Arshiya

Long pants? In this heat? Couldn’t be us. When even Europe’s melting and your phone screen fogs up from all the pap pics of celebs serving thigh on thigh - there’s only one logical move: shorts. More importantly shorts for men.

They never really left, but now they’re having a full-blown comeback moment. Shorts are the main character. The blueprint. The drip when the forecast hits unholy. Whether you’re chasing sunrises in Gokarna, café hopping in Bandra, or just pretending to check flight prices at 2AM - shorts = freedom.

They’re airy. They’re comfy. They give just enough thigh to keep things interesting (and Insta-thirsty).
2025 shorts aren’t lazy. They’re loud. Well-cut. Low-effort, high-reward. Basically, the perfect wingman.

And if you’re a Nobero guy - the kind who collects sunsets, playlists, and passport stamps - this is your sign to stock up, show off, and let the legs breathe.

Travel Shorts = Core Memory Creators

Welcome to the traveleisure era, where style meets utility - and then books a one-way ticket to Kasol.

Men are no longer picking between “I look great” and “I feel good.” Travel shorts today do both. Think drawstring waists, quick-dry fabrics, anti-odor tech, and deep enough pockets to actually carry things (like your passport, AirPods, and that suspiciously undercharged power bank).

  • Nobero Pick:

Ultra-comfort Travel Shorts - breathable, stretchable, and stitched to keep up with your wildest travel plans.

Oversized Everything (Shorts Edition)

2025 is not the year for tight thighs, bro. Oversized shorts are having a moment. Roomy, relaxed, and paired best with oversized tees (a Nobero fave), this trend screams airport flex or coastal chill.

Add a bucket hat, some slides, and you’re golden. Or better yet, grab your backpack, sling on some headphones, and start your slow-motion reel in the streets of Pondicherry.

  • Nobero Pro-tip:

Balance is key. Pair Nobero’s oversized shorts  with a slightly fitted tee or structured shacket.

Cargo Comeback

Yes, the pockets are back. And no, this isn’t 2004 — it’s 2025, but better.

Modern men’s cargo shorts are sleeker, more fitted, and way more aesthetic. They're a full-blown flex for the guy who wants functionality without looking like he just walked out of an army camp.

  • Best for:

Long drives to Lonavala, weekend treks in Himachal, or just city exploring on foot.

Earthy, Neutral, and Washed-out Hues

Shades like sage green, dusty beige, charcoal, and muted mustard are dominating the palette this year. Why? Because they blend into both the city and nature. They vibe with your desert safari and your brunch date.

  • Where to Wear:

Whether you’re café hopping in Jaipur, stargazing in Spiti, or chilling in Hampi, these tones just work.

  • Style Pairing:

Go tone-on-tone for a sleek ‘fit, or mix with a graphic oversized tee to add contrast.

Statement Shorts 

We see you, fashion daredevils. For you, shorts aren’t just shorts. They’re your canvas. Loud prints, bold patterns, graffiti vibes, and even neon trims are making headlines in 2025.

This isn’t subtle. This is a style that speaks up — without needing to shout.

  • Where they shine:

Music fests in Goa. Chill nights in Dharamkot. Beach bonfires in Gokarna. You get the vibe.

Athleisure Shorts with Street-Ready Edge

Sporty silhouettes are merging into everyday wear. Think track-style shorts, zippered pockets, mesh panels - all styled not with sneakers, but maybe with a linen shirt and sliders.

This is for the guy who might hit a trek at 6AM and a taproom by 8PM. One short, two lives.

  • When to Wear:

Airport looks, Sunday market strolls, or even morning hikes in Nainital.

Final Packing List - Nobero Style

Shorts aren’t just what you wear. They’re how you travel.

In 2025, men’s shorts are more intentional than ever - built for movement, for style, and for capturing memories in motion. At Nobero, we design them for the urban explorer, the weekend wanderer, and the guy who says yes to new plans without checking his calendar.

Whether you're layering with an oversized hoodie, pairing them with crisp graphic tees, or styling them up with a textured shacket, Nobero shorts are built to move with you.

TL;DR: Men’s Shorts Trends 2025

  • Travel-Ready Tech Shorts – Built for airport-to-hike transitions.

  • Oversized Cuts – For the laidback, loose-fit king.

  • Utility Cargos – Function meets flex.

  • Earthy Tones – The new travel uniform.

  • Athleisure x Streetwear – From treadmill to tapas bar.
