Top 5 Men's Shorts for a Stylish Summer Wardrobe

Mridu

Summer is that time of year when style takes a breezy, laid-back turn, and nothing beats the comfort of men’s shorts for staying cool and looking effortlessly trendy. Whether you're jet-setting to your dream beach destination, heading out for a casual brunch, or simply chilling at home, the right pair of shorts for summer can elevate your look without compromising on comfort. But let’s face it, navigating through the endless options can be overwhelming. From chino shorts that strike the perfect balance between smart and casual to relaxed cotton shorts that scream vacation vibes, picking the right pair can transform your summer wardrobe.

This guide breaks down the top 5 men's shorts for a stylish summer wardrobe, giving you foolproof outfit ideas for every occasion. Plus, with Nobero's versatile collection, you'll have no trouble staying on-trend while staying cool. So, whether you're looking for styling shorts for summer or searching for the top 10 men's shorts for summer, this curated list has got you covered.

1. Cargo Shorts: Functional Yet Fashionable

Cargo shorts have made a major comeback, blending utilitarian design with contemporary streetwear aesthetics. With their signature multiple pockets and relaxed fit, these shorts are perfect for when you need style with practicality.

How to Style:

  • Travel-ready Comfort: Pair khaki or olive cargo shorts with a knitted polo from our men’s textured polo collection and comfortable sneakers for an easygoing travel outfit.
  • Adventure Mode: Heading for an outdoor adventure? Throw on a breathable graphic tee with cargo shorts and hiking boots for a rugged, masculine look.
  • Weekend Chill: For a laid-back weekend vibe, pair your cargo shorts with an oversized T-shirt and chunky sneakers.

Best Season: Ideal for summer and transitional weather, Nobero’s cargo shorts for men offer breathability and ample storage for your essentials.

2. Chino Shorts: The Ultimate Smart-casual Essential

When it comes to striking the perfect balance between casual and formal, chino shorts are the undisputed champions. These versatile shorts come in breathable fabrics like cotton or twill, making them an excellent choice for warmer days when you need a more refined look without sacrificing comfort.

How to Style:

  • Brunch Ready: Pair beige or navy chino shorts with a crisp white linen shirt and loafers for a polished, daytime look.
  • Office-appropriate Summer Look: Opt for tailored chino shorts in neutral hues, paired with a lightweight blazer and a tucked-in polo shirt for a smart-casual vibe.
  • Date Night Perfection: Elevate your evening look by teaming dark-colored chino shorts with a button-down shirt and sleek sneakers.

Best Season: Perfect for spring and summer, chino shorts offer versatility for both day and night outings.

3. Athletic Shorts: Comfort Meets Functionality

Athletic shorts are no longer confined to the gym, they’ve become a staple in streetwear culture. Whether you’re hitting the gym or just lounging around, these gym shorts for men combine functionality with a cool, sporty aesthetic.

How to Style:

  • Gym-ready Vibe: Pair black athletic shorts with a moisture-wicking tank or a Nobero gym T-shirt and performance sneakers for a high-intensity workout look.
  • Athleisure Done Right: Opt for neutral-colored athletic shorts with an oversized hoodie and trendy sneakers for a relaxed, off-duty vibe.
  • Morning Jog Style: Keep it simple with a Nobero crew neck tee and running shoes to balance comfort and style.

Best Season: Perfect for summer and all-year-round athleisure looks.

4. Denim Shorts: The Classic Summer Staple

Nothing spells casual cool like a pair of denim shorts. Whether you’re heading to a beach party or exploring a new city, denim shorts give off a timeless vibe that’s equal parts stylish and relaxed. They’re easy to pair with almost anything in your wardrobe, making them a no-brainer for summer outings.

How to Style:

  • Beach Day Casual: Team distressed denim shorts with an oversized T-shirt for men and flip-flops for an effortlessly cool beach look.
  • Urban Explorer: Pair mid-wash denim shorts with Nobero’s travel hoodie and sneakers for an edgy, street-style aesthetic on a mountain vacation.
  • Laid-back Evening Look: For casual evening outings, throw on a well-fitted black T-shirt for men and minimal sneakers to keep things low-key yet stylish.

Best Season: Perfect for summer beach trips and warm-weather adventures.

5. Linen Shorts: Light and Breathable for Hot Days

Linen shorts are the ultimate saviors during those scorching summer days. Known for their lightweight, breathable fabric, these shorts keep you cool while exuding a laid-back elegance. They’re the ideal choice when you want to stay comfortable while maintaining a refined look.

How to Style:

  • Vacation Vibes: Pair beige or pastel linen shorts with a loose Cuban collar shirt or a relaxed-fit T-shirt and espadrilles for a resort-ready look.
  • Casual Beach Day: Opt for light-colored linen shorts paired with a breezy oversized tee and slide sandals.
  • Summer Date Look: Dress up linen shorts with a tucked-in textured polo shirt and loafers for a polished yet comfortable ensemble.

Best Season: Ideal for high summer, linen shorts ensure maximum comfort without compromising on style.
